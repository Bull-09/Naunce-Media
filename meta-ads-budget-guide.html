How much should you spend on Meta ads when you start?

By the Nuance Media teamUpdated 18 November 2026

Short answer

How much should you spend on Meta ads when you start?

Size your starting budget so each ad set can reach about 50 conversions a week, the number Meta uses to finish its learning phase. Weekly budget per ad set ≈ expected cost per conversion × 50.

Spend too little on Meta ads and the algorithm never learns who your buyers are. Spend too much too soon and you pay for the lesson twice. Here’s how we size a starting budget.

The learning phase sets the floor

Meta says an ad set needs roughly 50 optimisation events in 7 days (purchases, if you optimise for purchases) to leave the learning phase. Until then, delivery is unstable and costs bounce around.

Weekly budget per ad set ≈ expected cost per conversion × 50

A worked example

If you expect each purchase to cost around ₹500, one ad set needs about ₹500 × 50 = ₹25,000 a week, roughly ₹3,600 a day. Two ad sets need twice that.

If that number scares you

  • Use fewer ad sets. One well-fed ad set beats five starving ones.
  • Optimise for an earlier event. Add-to-cart or initiate-checkout happen more often than purchases, so the algorithm learns faster. Move to purchases once volume allows.
  • Use broad audiences. Let Meta find buyers instead of slicing your budget into tiny interest groups.
  • Spend on creative, not targeting. On Meta today, the ad itself does most of the targeting.

Leave room to test creatives

Ads wear out. We plan a new batch every month and keep part of the budget for testing new hooks and angles, so there’s always a fresh winner ready when the old one fades.

What about lead generation?

Same maths with cost per lead. For one corporate gifting brand, leads cost ₹58 on average, so 50 leads a week needed roughly ₹2,900. See how that campaign worked.

The short version

  1. Estimate your cost per conversion.
  2. Multiply by 50 for a weekly budget per ad set.
  3. Fewer ad sets, broad audiences, fresh creatives every month.

Not sure what your cost per conversion will be? Tell us your product and margins and we’ll give you an honest estimate, including “don’t start yet” if that’s the answer.

Quick answers.

How much should I spend on Meta ads per day?

Enough for each ad set to get about 50 conversions a week. Multiply your expected cost per conversion by 50, then divide by 7. At ₹500 per purchase that is about ₹3,600 a day per ad set.

What is the Meta ads learning phase?

The period after launch or a major edit when Meta is still learning who to show your ads to. Meta says an ad set needs roughly 50 optimisation events in 7 days to exit it.

Can I start Meta ads with a small budget?

Yes, with fewer ad sets, broad audiences and, if needed, an earlier optimisation event like add-to-cart. Just expect slower learning.

How often should I change my ad creatives?

We plan new creatives every month and test new hooks continuously, because ads wear out as the same people see them repeatedly.
