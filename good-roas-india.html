What is a good ROAS for D2C brands in India?

By the Nuance Media teamUpdated 18 November 2026

Short answer

What is a good ROAS for D2C brands in India?

There is no universal good ROAS. A good ROAS is any ROAS above your break-even ROAS, which is 1 divided by your margin after product, shipping, payment and return costs.

“What’s a good ROAS?” is the question we hear most. The honest answer: it depends on your margins. A 3x ROAS can make one brand rich and quietly bankrupt another.

Start with your break-even ROAS

ROAS (return on ad spend) is revenue divided by ad spend. Your break-even ROAS is the ROAS at which ads neither make nor lose money:

Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ margin after costs

“Margin after costs” means what’s left from each sale after the product, shipping, payment gateway fees and an allowance for returns. In India, include RTO (return to origin) on cash-on-delivery orders; it is easy to forget and it is expensive.

A worked example

Say you sell a product for ₹1,000. The product costs ₹350, shipping ₹80, payment fees ₹20, and you set aside ₹50 for returns and RTO. That leaves ₹500, a 50% margin. Your break-even ROAS is 1 ÷ 0.5 = 2x.

Same product, same ads: the difference between 2x and 3x is the difference between a hobby and a business.

Why the ROAS in Ads Manager can mislead you

  • Attribution. Meta credits sales within its attribution window. Some of those buyers would have bought anyway, and some sales Meta caused it never sees.
  • Cash on delivery. Ads Manager counts the order, not the delivered, paid order. High RTO means your real ROAS is lower than the dashboard says.
  • Repeat customers. If people come back and buy again, a first-order ROAS near break-even can still be very profitable over time.

That’s why we also track blended ROAS: total revenue divided by total ad spend across every account and channel. It’s harder to flatter.

What we’ve seen

For one wellness brand with healthy margins we reached 7.11x ROAS in a single month, and 6.06x blended across its two ad accounts. That’s a strong result for that brand’s margins, not a target for yours. Read the case study.

The short version

  1. Work out your margin after product, shipping, fees and returns.
  2. Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ that margin.
  3. Judge campaigns on profit above break-even, and watch blended ROAS.

Quick answers.

What is ROAS?

ROAS (return on ad spend) is the revenue your ads generate divided by what you spent on them. ₹3 of revenue for every ₹1 spent is a 3x ROAS.

What is a good ROAS for Meta ads in India?

Any ROAS above your break-even ROAS. Calculate it as 1 divided by your margin after product, shipping, payment fees and returns. With a 50% margin, break-even is 2x.

What is blended ROAS?

Total revenue divided by total ad spend across all channels and accounts. It is harder to inflate than a single campaign’s ROAS.

Why is my real profit lower than my Meta ROAS suggests?

Usually returns, RTO on cash-on-delivery orders, and attribution: Ads Manager counts orders inside its attribution window, not delivered and paid orders.
