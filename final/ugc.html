UGC questions.

What is a UGC video ad?

A UGC (user-generated content) video ad is a short video where a real person talks about or uses a product on camera, in the style of a normal social post. It feels native in the feed, so people watch it instead of skipping it. Brands run these videos as Meta ads.

Do UGC ads still work in 2026?

Yes, when they are scripted for selling. A good UGC ad hooks in the first three seconds, shows the problem and the proof, and ends with a clear offer. Shooting several hooks per concept lets Meta show you which opener works.

How many videos do I get from one shoot?

Each concept is shot with several different hooks, so one shoot produces multiple ad versions to test. Exact numbers depend on the brief; we agree them before the shoot.

Can I use the videos on my website and social pages?

Yes, within the usage agreed for the creators on your project. We confirm usage rights before the shoot so there are no surprises.

What formats do you deliver?

9:16 for Reels and Stories, 4:5 and 1:1 for feeds, with captions burned in for sound-off scrolling.
