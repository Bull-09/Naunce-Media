3.81x ROAS on a test account

A second account to test audiences and creatives without disturbing the winners.

Summary

What happened in this case?

A second Meta ad account for a wellness D2C brand returned 3.81x ROAS (₹23.3 lakh revenue from ₹6.1 lakh spend) while testing audiences and creatives; both accounts together ran at 6.06x blended ROAS.

The problem

The same wellness brand needed room to test new audiences and creative angles without risking the campaigns that were already selling.

What we did

  • Opened a second ad account as a testing ground.
  • Split prospecting and retargeting into separate campaigns with separate budgets.
  • Tested new creative angles here first, and promoted only the winners to the main account.

What we learned

A test account that still returns 3.81x is cheap research. Together the two accounts ran at a 6.06x blended ROAS for the month.
