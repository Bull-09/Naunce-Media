01 Wellness D2C

Always-on sales

A wellness brand selling online. One Meta ad account with a sales campaign for each product, plus small seasonal offers.

7.11xROAS in one month

What we did

  • Product-level sales campaigns that each get their own budget.
  • Seasonal offers kept small and sharp, scaled only after they worked.
  • A weekly report from Ads Manager, read together on a call.
  • ₹92.9 lakh revenue
  • 2,587 purchases
  • ₹505 per purchase
  • 13.5 lakh people reached

02 Wellness D2C

Retargeting and testing

A second ad account built to test new audiences and retargeting without disturbing the winners.

3.81xROAS on a test account

What we did

  • Prospecting and retargeting split into separate campaigns.
  • New creative angles tested here first, winners promoted to the main account.
  • ₹23.3 lakh revenue
  • 776 purchases
  • ₹6.1 lakh spend
  • 8.2 lakh people reached

03 Corporate gifting

Leads, not likes

A festive corporate gifting push. The goal was conversations and enquiries, so we measured cost per lead.

₹58average cost per lead

What we did

  • Separate campaigns for WhatsApp chats, a lead form and awareness.
  • Week two doubled down on what worked, and form leads went from 1 to 36.
  • 203 leads in 2 weeks
  • 166 WhatsApp chats
  • 37 form leads
  • 6.9 lakh people reached

  • Offer template built
