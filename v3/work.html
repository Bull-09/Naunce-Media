01 D2C wellness | Sales campaigns

Cannazo India

A wellness D2C brand selling drops, gummies and extracts online. We run two Meta ad accounts with a sales campaign per product, plus seasonal offers.

Revenue, primary account1 to 31 Aug 2026
ROASOn ₹13.06 lakh spend

What the reports show

August across both accounts: ₹19.17 lakh spent, ₹1.16 crore in revenue, 3,363 purchases. September to the 28th on the primary account: ₹9.42 lakh spent, ₹52.5 lakh in revenue, 5.58x.

  • Always-on sales campaigns per product: Berberine, Uplift Drops, Calm Drops, Uplift Extract, Uplift Gummies.
  • Seasonal offers kept small and sharp: a birthday offer returned 36.61x on ₹4,757, Independence Day sale 18.90x on ₹15,433.
  • A second account to test new prospecting and retargeting without disturbing the winners.

02 Corporate gifting | Lead generation

Bake Hub by Ritika

A Diwali corporate gifting campaign. The goal was conversations and leads, not direct sales, so we measured cost per lead and WhatsApp conversation.

Leads in 2 weeks166 WhatsApp chats and 37 forms
Average cost per leadOn ₹13,646 total spend
People reached14 to 28 Sep 2026

Built for conversations

Four campaigns split by objective: WhatsApp conversations, a lead form, and a brand-awareness layer on top. Week two doubled down on what worked and the form leads jumped from 1 to 36.

  • WhatsApp offers at about ₹48 per conversation.
  • Lead form at ₹104 per lead, awareness at under ₹0.10 per recall lift.
