Short answer

Who runs Nuance Media?

Nuance Media is a performance marketing agency co-founded by Nishant Sinha and Lokesh Shinkar, with teams in Surat and Mumbai. It runs Meta ads, makes UGC videos and ad creatives, and builds websites with SEO for D2C, B2B and ecommerce businesses, with one goal: increasing their sales.

Why we work like employees.

Agencies usually behave like vendors: deliver the deliverables, send the invoice, defend the report. Employees behave differently. They care whether the company actually grew this month.

So that’s how we work. We sit in your Slack or WhatsApp. We learn your margins, your best sellers and your slow months. We make the ads, run them and fix the page they land on, and every week we answer to one number: sales, or leads if you’re B2B.

The founders are on the work, not just on the pitch. Behind them is a team of media buyers, designers, editors and a roster of creators across Surat and Mumbai.

Our ethics.

Eight rules we work by. If we ever break one, tell us. Loudly.

  • Real numbers only.

    Every result we publish comes from Ads Manager. We don’t round up, and we don’t count reach as revenue.

  • Your account, your data.

    Ad accounts, pixels, pages and creatives stay in your business. We work through partner access, so you can see everything, always.

  • We’ll tell you when the budget is too small.

    Even if it costs us the deal. Wasting your money to keep a retainer isn’t a business model we want.

  • Nothing fake.

    No bought followers, fake reviews or bot engagement. No ad claims your product can’t back up.

  • Bad weeks get reported too.

    You see every week, with what went wrong and what we’re changing. Silence is not a reporting format.

  • Client names stay private.

    We don’t name-drop clients without their OK, and we only show creatives with permission.

  • Honest scope.

    We tell you what we don’t do before you sign, so nobody pays for something we were never going to deliver.

  • We spend it like it’s ours.

    Every rupee of ad spend has to earn its place. If an ad doesn’t sell, it goes, however much we like it.

And the things we won’t do.

  • Send a 40-slide deck to explain a 2% CTR.

  • Celebrate impressions while your sales sit still.

  • Promise you’ll “go viral”. Nobody can. Anyone who says so is guessing.

  • Keep running an ad because we like it. If it doesn’t sell, it goes.

  • Reply “noted 👍” and vanish for a week.

  • Say “synergy” with a straight face.
