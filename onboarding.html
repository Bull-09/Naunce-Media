Client onboarding

Welcome aboard. Here’s the checklist.

For brands that have just signed. Send these over and we can start the audit the same week.

Access we need

  • Partner access to your Meta Business ManagerWe send a request from our business; you approve it. You keep ownership.
  • Ad account access, with permission to manage campaigns
  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API accessOr your developer’s contact, so we can check events together.
  • Website or Shopify staff accessOnly what’s needed for tracking and landing pages.
  • Google Analytics or GA4 access, if you use it

Information and assets

  • Product list with prices and rough marginsThis is how we work out your break-even ROAS.
  • Best sellers and slow movers
  • Logo, brand colours, fonts and any brand guidelines
  • Past ads that worked, and ones that didn’t
  • Offers and sale dates for the next three months
  • Who on your side approves creatives, and how fast

Your first 30 days.

  1. Week 1: Audit and plan

    Tracking, ad account, past creatives and margins reviewed. You get a written plan with the numbers we’ll hold ourselves to.

  2. Week 2: Creative sprint

    Scripts and static concepts sent for your approval, then UGC shoots.

  3. Week 3: Launch

    Campaigns go live by product and funnel stage. We check results daily for the first two weeks.

  4. Week 4: First report

    Spend, purchases, revenue and ROAS by campaign, plus what we learned and what we test next.
