Website & SEO questions.

Why does a landing page matter for Meta ads?

Because the ad only earns the click. If the page is slow, confusing or doesn’t match the ad, people leave and you paid for nothing. A fast page that continues the ad’s promise lowers your cost per purchase without touching the ad.

Do you build on Shopify?

Yes. We build Shopify stores for D2C brands and custom websites for B2B and service businesses.

What does your SEO include?

Technical SEO (speed, indexing, structured data), on-page SEO (titles, headings, internal links) and content that answers what your buyers search for, written so search engines and AI assistants can understand and quote it.

How long does SEO take?

Usually months, not weeks. Technical fixes can help quickly; content and authority build over time. That’s why we pair SEO with Meta ads that sell from week three.

How long does a website take?

A focused landing page is quicker than a full store. We agree the scope and the timeline in writing before we start.
