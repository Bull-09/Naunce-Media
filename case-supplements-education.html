Case study Supplements

Benefits you get in one glance

Turning benefits into clean, scannable ads that answer the buyer in a glance.

Summary

What happened in this case?

Nuance Media made benefit-led static ads for a supplements brand, with one benefit per ad and different angles for cold and warm audiences.

The problem

Supplements need explaining, and nobody reads a paragraph in a feed.

What we did

  • Put one benefit in each ad, so the winning message is obvious in the results.
  • Used problem-first angles for cold audiences and product-first angles for warm ones.

What we learned

When every ad tests one idea, the results tell you what to say next.
