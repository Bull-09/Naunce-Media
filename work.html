About these numbers.

Where do your numbers come from?

From the client’s own Meta Ads Manager reports for the months shown. ROAS is revenue divided by ad spend, as Meta reports it.

Why are client names hidden?

Most brands don’t want competitors to know who runs their ads or what they spend. We share more detail on a call where we can.

Some cases have no numbers. Why?

Those were creative and strategy projects: audits, plans, templates and ad design. We only publish ad results we can show from Ads Manager.
