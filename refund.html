This policy applies to services bought from Nuance Media. Where a signed agreement says something different, the agreement wins.

Ad spend

Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta, Google or other platforms. We never hold it, so we can’t refund it. Platform refunds follow each platform’s own policy.

Monthly retainers (Meta ads management)

You can cancel with [X] days’ written notice. Fees for the current month are not refundable once work has started.

UGC videos and creatives

Once scripts are approved and a shoot is booked, creator and production costs are committed and can’t be refunded. If we haven’t started, we refund any advance minus costs already incurred.

Websites

Website projects are billed in milestones. Completed milestones are not refundable. If you cancel, you pay for work done to date and receive that work.
